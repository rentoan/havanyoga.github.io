Privacy

HAVAN’s current static website does not provide accounts, contact forms, newsletters or payments. HAVAN uses optional Google Analytics only when you select “Allow.”

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics helps us understand page visits and interactions such as opening a Journal article, viewing a program guide or following an external provider link. The measurement ID is G-FVLMZX34P9. Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization remain disabled.

Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. If you decline, HAVAN does not load the Google Analytics script. You can change your choice at any time.

Affiliate links

Some product links are ClickBank affiliate links and include a Tracking ID identifying the link placement. When you follow one, ClickBank and the product provider may set cookies or process technical and referral information under their own privacy policies. HAVAN may receive reports about clicks, referrals, transactions and commissions, but does not receive your payment-card details from these links.

External links

When you follow a link to another website, that service may process data under its own privacy policy. HAVAN does not control external sites.

Embedded video

HAVAN uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed mode. The video player is not loaded until you choose “Load video.” Once loaded, YouTube may process technical information and set or access data according to Google’s privacy terms. You can use the creator link instead if you prefer to watch directly on YouTube.

Hosting data

GitHub Pages and infrastructure providers may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the website. Review GitHub’s privacy documentation for details relevant to Pages hosting.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the site’s data practices change.
