Privacy

HAVAN’s current static website does not provide accounts, contact forms, newsletters, payments, first-party analytics or advertising pixels. HAVAN does not intentionally collect personal information through this site.

Affiliate links

Some product links are ClickBank affiliate links and include a Tracking ID identifying the link placement. When you follow one, ClickBank and the product provider may set cookies or process technical and referral information under their own privacy policies. HAVAN may receive reports about clicks, referrals, transactions and commissions, but does not receive your payment-card details from these links.

External links

When you follow a link to another website, that service may process data under its own privacy policy. HAVAN does not control external sites.

Hosting data

GitHub Pages and infrastructure providers may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the website. Review GitHub’s privacy documentation for details relevant to Pages hosting.

Changes

This notice will be updated before adding first-party analytics, forms, advertising pixels or other tools that change how data is handled.
