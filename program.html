Hyperbolic Stretching: what to know before you decide

A careful look at The FlexTense Method based on publicly available provider information—not a first-person review.

HAVAN has not purchased or completed this program. Features described below are claims made by the provider and have not been independently tested by us. The outbound link is not currently an affiliate link.

What the provider presents

The sales page describes a home video program focused on stretching and flexibility, with routines advertised as taking about eight minutes. It presents material ranging from beginner to advanced levels and content for several muscle groups and movement goals.

What we cannot confirm yet

  • The quality and sequence of the lessons inside the paid program.
  • Whether the beginner material will suit your individual starting point.
  • Available languages, captions and accessibility features.
  • How exercises can be adapted for individual needs.
  • Any specific health or pain-related outcome.

Questions to check at checkout

  1. What is the final price in your currency?
  2. Is access one-time, subscription-based or time limited?
  3. Which refund period and conditions are shown at checkout?
  4. What equipment, space and weekly time are expected?
  5. Can you contact support before buying?

Prices, bonuses and terms can change. Read the current sales page and checkout terms before paying.
