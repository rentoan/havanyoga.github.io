About HAVAN

Movement guidance with room for your own judgment.

HAVAN is an independent English-language editorial site for people exploring gentle movement and at-home programs around a busy workday.

Our approach

We aim to make choices easier to understand. That means practical starting points, clear questions before a purchase and honest limits around what we know. We do not assume one routine will work for every body.

Our standards

We distinguish provider claims from our own verification, do not invent personal experience, and avoid guaranteed health outcomes. HAVAN content is educational and is not a substitute for personal medical advice.
