What to check before buying a home movement program

A sales page can create urgency. Your checklist can create space to decide.

Format and access

  • Streaming, downloads, live sessions—or a mix?
  • Lifetime access or a subscription?
  • Captions, transcripts and language options?
  • Phone and desktop compatibility?

Fit and progression

  • Who is the stated starting level?
  • Are modifications demonstrated?
  • How long and frequent are the sessions?
  • What space or equipment is required?

Price, support and refunds

  • Confirm the final checkout total and currency.
  • Read the refund window and its conditions—not just the headline.
  • Check how support works and who provides it.
  • Save the terms and receipt shown when you buy.
Be cautious with guaranteed health results, countdown timers that repeatedly reset, and missing contact or refund details.
